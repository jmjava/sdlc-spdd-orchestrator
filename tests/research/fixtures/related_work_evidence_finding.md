# Related work and novelty

## Novelty

> A repository-native process model that makes intent (C-DRIFT), process
> compliance (C-COMPLY), and context load (C-CONTEXT) observable, with evidence
> that the hybrid reduces drift.

## What we are not claiming

Not a new LLM, not a new RAG theory, not a compiled multi-agent runtime.

Guide is **fork-only**.
